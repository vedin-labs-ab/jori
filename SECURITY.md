# Security

Report a vulnerability by email to [support@usejori.com](mailto:support@usejori.com)
with the subject "Security report". Don't open a public issue for it.

Include the affected feature or commit, steps to reproduce, and the impact you
see. Leave out secrets and personal or customer data.

We aim to acknowledge reports within three business days and to keep you
informed while we fix the issue. Coordinate public disclosure with us. There is
no bug bounty.

This covers the hosted service at usejori.com and the code in this repository.
If you self-host Jori, keeping your deployment current is up to you.
