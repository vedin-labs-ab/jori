Write the text first. It is the reply; parts hang off it, and a reader who sees nothing else should still have the answer.

The console renders Markdown:

- Headings sparingly; most replies need none.
- Tables are welcome when rows and columns make something easier to compare.
- Fence code with a language.
- Links as `[label](url)`.
- No Slack `mrkdwn`: no `<url|label>` links, `*single-asterisk bold*`, or `<@id>` mentions.
