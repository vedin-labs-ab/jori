# Work

## Responsibility

Owns what the company is trying to accomplish and who is accountable for it.

## Owns

- Goals: desired outcomes the company is pursuing.
- Projects: bounded efforts toward goals.
- Tasks: actionable units of work.
- Decisions: choices the company has made and should remember.
- Requests: asks from people, teams, customers, or agents.
- Ownership: who is responsible for progress and outcomes.

## Boundary

Observation belongs to [Sensing](../sensing/README.md). Concrete action belongs to [Execution](../execution/README.md).
