# Identity

## Responsibility

Owns the organization boundary, people, and permissions that determine who can use Milo and what they can see, trigger, delegate, approve, or change.

## Owns

- [Organizations](./organizations.md)
- [Users](./users.md)
- [Permissions](./permissions.md)

## Boundary

Agent identity and behavior belong to [Agents](../agents/index.md). Requests, tasks, runs, and review points belong to [Runs](../runs/index.md).
