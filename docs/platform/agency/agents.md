# Agents

## Responsibility

Owns the AI teammates Milo can delegate to, including their mandate, skills, plans, tools, and accountability for outcomes.

## Owns

- Agent profiles: named or configured AI workers.
- Delegations: responsibilities assigned from people, systems, or workflows.
- Plans: proposed paths from request to outcome.
- Skills: reusable ways of doing work.
- Tools: intentional use of integrations, models, and execution environments.

## Boundary

Workflow checkpoints belong to [Workflows](../workflows/index.md). Permission assignment and policy enforcement belong to [Company](../company/index.md).
