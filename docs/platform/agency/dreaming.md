# Dreaming

## Responsibility

Owns background reflection that finds useful opportunities before a person explicitly asks.

## Owns

- Reflection over company context, activity, work, and outcomes.
- Pattern detection across repeated signals, friction, and manual effort.
- Opportunities where Milo could help, automate, summarize, route, or prepare.
- Draft suggestions that wait for review, activation, or dismissal.

## Boundary

Activation belongs to [Workflows](../workflows/index.md). Work ownership belongs to [Work](../work/index.md). Approval belongs to [Company](../company/index.md).
