# Sources

## Responsibility

Owns raw company inputs before they become memory, work, triggers, or agent context.

## Includes

- Messages, email, comments, and threads.
- Meetings and live conversations.
- Files, documents, code, notes, and other artifacts.
- Imports, migrations, and connected-tool activity.

## Boundary

Interpretation belongs to [Memory](./memory.md). Evidence tracking belongs to [Provenance](./provenance.md).
