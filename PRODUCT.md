# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Founders and leads at small software companies (5–50 people) who live in meetings, email, and Slack. The landing page reader is a founder/CEO deciding whether to trust an AI teammate with their inbox and calendar; the console user is that same person (and later their team) supervising Milo's work. Technical evaluators read closely for what Milo can access and when it acts.

## Product Purpose

Milo is an AI teammate that works inside an organization's existing tools: Slack, GitHub, and Linear for conversation; Gmail/Outlook, Google/Microsoft Calendar, and Notion for reading and writing. It runs scheduled playbooks (morning brief, meeting prep, follow-up sweep, week in review), answers mentions in threads, learns the organization's workstreams from real activity, and takes consequential actions only through ask-first approvals with visible run receipts. Success: users start the day already caught up, and trust Milo enough to expand it across the team.

## Brand Personality

Sharp, easygoing, trustworthy. Milo's own voice prompt is the brand voice: direct and compact, contractions and plain words, dry wit when the moment offers it, never performed enthusiasm. Calm confidence over hype. Specificity over adjectives: exact times, named tools, receipts. Slightly playful and physically tactile (the pressed-button depth in the design system), never loud.

## Anti-references

- Generic 2026 AI-agent marketing: gradient mesh, glassmorphism, neon-on-black, floating 3D shapes, bento grids of vague features, logo walls as the only proof.
- Hype claims ("10x productivity", "replace your team"), invented metrics, fake logos or testimonials.
- Enterprise-suite coldness: Solutions dropdowns, stock office photography.
- Em dashes anywhere in copy (banned by Milo's voice rules).

## Design Principles

- Specificity is proof: real cadences, real artifacts, real approval flows in every prop; never vague claims.
- The work product is the hero: show briefs, threads, and receipts, not admin chrome.
- Trust is a feature: approvals, scopes, and receipts get designed as prominently as capabilities.
- Personality through copy and micro-physics, not decoration: quiet canvas, a few deliberate moments.
- Marketing and product share one language (organization, runs, playbooks, ask-first) and one design system.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA. Respect prefers-reduced-motion: every page must fully work without motion. Semantic landmarks and heading order. Visible focus states come from the design system. Plain-language copy.
