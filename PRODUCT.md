# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Company operators and teams configuring Milo to help with work across Slack,
connected tools, automations, approvals, and execution review. They are usually
in a focused setup or monitoring workflow and need the console to feel fast,
predictable, and trustworthy.

## Product Purpose

Milo provides AI teammates for company work. The console lets teams connect
integrations, define automations and skills, manage permissions, and inspect
runs so Milo can move work forward with the right context and controls.

## Brand Personality

Calm, capable, operational. The product should feel direct and competent rather
than decorative, with copy and interface choices that help users complete work
without ceremony.

## Anti-references

Avoid marketing-heavy SaaS dashboards, decorative gradients, noisy motion,
invented controls, glassmorphism, and overdesigned card grids. Do not obscure
standard product affordances for visual novelty.

## Design Principles

- Keep the task primary: navigation, forms, lists, and approvals should be easy
  to scan and act on.
- Earn trust through restraint: use familiar shadcn/ui patterns and consistent
  states rather than bespoke flourishes.
- Make performance visible: the console should load only the code a route needs
  and avoid work that does not support the current task.
- Show control clearly: integrations, permissions, automations, and runs should
  communicate state and consequence before action.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA behavior, including keyboard access, visible focus, semantic
labels, readable contrast, and reduced-motion-safe interactions.
