# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Teams that use Milo to coordinate company work through Slack, connected tools,
automations, artifacts, and run history. Users are in operational workflows:
connecting integrations, reviewing runs, approving requests, inspecting
artifacts, setting permissions, and teaching Milo how their organization works.

## Product Purpose

Milo provides AI teammates for company work. The product watches relevant
conversations, gathers context, executes long-running tasks, and keeps teams in
control through an authenticated console for setup, monitoring, permissions,
automations, skills, integrations, and artifacts.

## Brand Personality

Calm, capable, operational, and direct. The interface should feel trustworthy,
quiet, and fast, with familiar product patterns that disappear into the task.

## Anti-references

Avoid marketing-style heroes inside task surfaces, decorative gradients,
glassmorphism, overdesigned card grids, fake metrics, generic dashboard filler,
repeated icon-heading-description blocks, modals as the first solution, and
decorative motion.

## Design Principles

- Start from the user outcome, not the component layout.
- Make the useful thing obvious before exposing secondary controls.
- Prefer dense, readable product surfaces for comparison, review, and scanning.
- Use restrained color only for state, selection, and primary actions.
- Keep copy short, concrete, and non-promotional.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keep keyboard access, visible focus, semantic labels, readable contrast, loading
states, error states, empty states, and reduced-motion considerations built into
the design rather than deferred to a cleanup pass.
