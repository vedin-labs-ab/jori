# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Founders, engineering leads, and whoever ended up running operations at software companies of roughly 10–80 people. They live in Slack, GitHub, and Linear, and they carry a list of recurring work that only happens because somebody remembers it: the release checklist, the dependency sweep, the on-call handover, the weekly triage, the customer bug that has to reach an issue. The landing page reader is that person recognizing their own list; the console user is the same person supervising Jori's runs and opening the apps Jori built for the team. Technical evaluators read closely for what Jori can access and when it acts.

## Product Purpose

Jori runs the recurring work a team does by hand, inside the tools that work already lives in: Slack, GitHub, and Linear for conversation and activity; Gmail/Outlook, Google/Microsoft Calendar, and Notion for reading and writing. When work repeats, Jori builds the team a real app for it: a live shared surface with typed durable state, kept current by a scheduled or event-driven run, that anyone on the team can open. Consequential actions go through ask-first approvals, and every run leaves receipts. Success: a team stops running a process out of one person's head, and starts opening the app Jori keeps current.

## Brand Personality

Sharp, easygoing, trustworthy. Jori's own voice prompt is the brand voice: direct and compact, contractions and plain words, dry wit when the moment offers it, never performed enthusiasm. Calm confidence over hype. Specificity over adjectives: exact cadences, named tools, receipts. Slightly playful and physically tactile (the pressed-button depth in the design system), never loud.

## Anti-references

- Generic 2026 AI-agent marketing: gradient mesh, glassmorphism, neon-on-black, floating 3D shapes, bento grids of vague features, logo walls as the only proof.
- Hype claims ("10x productivity", "replace your team"), invented metrics, fake logos or testimonials.
- Vibe-coding aesthetics: a prompt box as the hero, "describe your app" demos, generated-in-seconds theatre. Jori's apps are infrastructure a team depends on, not disposable output.
- Enterprise-suite coldness: Solutions dropdowns, stock office photography.
- Em dashes anywhere in copy (banned by Jori's voice rules).

## Design Principles

- Specificity is proof: real cadences, real apps, real approval flows in every prop; never vague claims.
- The app is the hero: show the live shared surface a team opens, its state, and the run that keeps it current. Not admin chrome, not chat transcripts.
- Trust is a feature: approvals, scopes, and receipts get designed as prominently as capabilities.
- Claim only what ships: no capability, region, or guarantee reaches copy before it is real and verifiable.
- Personality through copy and micro-physics, not decoration: quiet canvas, a few deliberate moments.
- Marketing and product share one language (organization, runs, apps, playbooks, ask-first) and one design system.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA. Respect prefers-reduced-motion: every page must fully work without motion. Semantic landmarks and heading order. Visible focus states come from the design system. Plain-language copy.
